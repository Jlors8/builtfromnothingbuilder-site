Privacy Policy

What the BuiltFromNothingBuilder application accesses, and what it does not.

§1 Overview

BuiltFromNothingBuilder is a single-operator application. It is not a public service: it does not have end users other than its developer, does not run a website that visitors sign into, and does not collect information from anyone other than its own operator.

§2 What this application accesses

DataWhy
A TikTok OAuth access token and refresh token, scoped to video.publishLets the application post videos to the developer's own authorized TikTok account
The developer's TikTok Open IDIdentifies which account a token belongs to, as returned by TikTok's own OAuth flow

§3 What this application does not do

  • Does not collect information from any TikTok user other than its own developer-operator
  • Does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking — there is no web frontend for anyone to visit
  • Does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • Does not access TikTok direct messages, follower lists, or any data outside the video.publish scope

§4 Storage and retention

Tokens are stored locally, in a file on the developer's own computer, for as long as the application is in use. They are never transmitted anywhere except directly to TikTok's own API. Revoking the application's access from TikTok's account settings, or deleting that local file, removes the application's ability to post immediately.

§5 Children's privacy

This application is not directed at children and is not intended to collect information from anyone. It is a single-operator tool operated by its developer only.

In plain terms: the only "data" this application handles is its own developer's login tokens, kept on the developer's own machine, used only to post the developer's own videos. There's no database of other people's information anywhere in it.

§6 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's scope changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
