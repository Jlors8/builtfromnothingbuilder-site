Terms of Service

These terms cover the BuiltFromNothingBuilder application and its use of TikTok's Content Posting API.

§1 What this application is

BuiltFromNothingBuilder is a personal, single-operator application. It generates short AI-made construction-timelapse videos and posts them to one TikTok account, which the developer owns and operates. It is not offered as a service to the public and does not accept sign-ups from other TikTok users.

§2 Scope of use

The application acts only on the developer's own authorized TikTok account, using the video.publish scope granted through TikTok's OAuth flow. It does not act on behalf of, or access data belonging to, any other TikTok account or user.

§3 Content and disclosure

Every video posted by this application is generated with AI tools, start to finish. Each post is labeled as AI-generated content using TikTok's is_aigc disclosure flag at the time of publishing, in line with TikTok's policy on synthetic media.

§4 Acceptable use

This application is operated in line with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and Content Posting API policies, including restrictions on posting frequency, content originality, and required disclosures.

§7 Changes

These terms may be updated as the application's scope changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

In plain terms: this is a hobby project run by one person, posting their own AI-generated videos to their own account. It isn't a multi-user platform, and these terms exist mainly to satisfy TikTok's developer registration requirements honestly.
